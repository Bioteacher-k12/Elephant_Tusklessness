Inheritance Note & X-Linked Inheritance Diagram

Inheritance note for the next question: In African elephants, the DNA that causes tusklessness is inherited differently by males and females—it is fatal to male embryos before birth, whereas female calves survive and inherit the tuskless trait.

Offspring Inheritance Outcomes: Tuskless Female (XTX) × Tusked Male (XY)

Female (XTX)
✓ Tuskless Female

Inherits the dominant tuskless allele (XT) and survives without tusks.

Female (XX)
✓ Tusked Female

Inherits normal X chromosomes from both parents and develops tusks.

Male (XTY)
✕ Embryonic Lethal / Does Not Survive

Inherits XT without a second X chromosome; fatal before birth.

Male (XY)
✓ Tusked Male

Inherits a normal X chromosome and survives with tusks.

Key Term: Selective Pressure An environmental factor (like predators, disease, or hunting) that affects which organisms survive and reproduce.
Key Term: Selective Advantage An inherited trait (in DNA) that helps an organism survive and reproduce better than individuals without it.
